The Deep Clean
Checklist

Room by room. In the right order. So you stop cleaning the same things twice.

1 Declutter first, then clean
2 Top to bottom, dry to wet
3 One zone at a time
4 Check as you go
Before You Start

The order you clean in matters more than how hard you clean.

Most people clean whatever bothers them most — which means they jump between rooms, repeat tasks, and exhaust themselves without finishing anything. This checklist gives you the exact sequence I follow in every Denver home I work in. One zone. Done right. Then the next.

Zone 01 · Start here
Kitchen
Clear every counter completely
Everything back in its home before you wipe a single surface.
Wipe counters and backsplash top to bottom
Use a degreaser for the area around the stove.
Clean stovetop and burner grates
Soak grates while you clean the rest — it makes the scrubbing easier.
Wipe inside and outside of microwave
A bowl of water + lemon in the microwave for 2 minutes loosens everything.
Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
Grease builds up here silently. Don't skip it.
Clean inside the fridge — toss expired items first
Edit before you wipe. Less stuff = faster cleaning.
Scrub the sink and polish the faucet
The sink is the last thing you clean in the kitchen — it gets everything else's residue.
Sweep and mop the floor
Always last in every room. Dust and crumbs fall as you clean above.
Lorena
"I never clean a kitchen that hasn't been decluttered first. Moving clutter around a dirty counter isn't cleaning — it's rearranging."
🚿
Zone 02 · Most satisfying
Bathrooms
Clear and wipe the countertop completely
Every product back in its drawer. No exceptions.
Apply toilet cleaner — let it sit while you clean the rest
Start the cleaner first so it does the work while you move on.
Wipe mirror top to bottom
Microfiber cloth + glass cleaner. No paper towels — they leave streaks.
Scrub the sink and faucet
Toothpaste residue around the faucet base — don't skip it.
Scrub the toilet — outside first, inside last
Lid, tank, base, seat, then bowl. Always in that order.
Scrub shower or tub walls and floor
Spray and let sit for 3 minutes before scrubbing — cuts your effort in half.
Empty trash and replace liner
Every bathroom. Every time.
Mop or wipe the floor
Last step. Always.
Lorena
"Apply the toilet cleaner first thing when you walk in. By the time you've cleaned everything else, it's ready to scrub. You'll save 5 minutes every single time."
🛏
Zone 03 · Where you rest
Bedrooms
Put away everything that doesn't belong in this room
Return items to their actual homes before you touch a cleaning cloth.
Strip the bed and wash all bedding
Pillowcases, sheets, duvet cover. All of it.
Dust surfaces top to bottom
Ceiling fans first, then shelves, then nightstands. Dust falls — always work down.
Wipe nightstands and dresser tops
Clear them first. Only put back what actually belongs there.
Clean mirrors and glass surfaces
Microfiber cloth, top to bottom.
Vacuum or sweep the floor including under the bed
Under the bed is where dust bunnies retire. Don't ignore it.
Make the bed with clean sheets
This is the last step. It transforms how the whole room feels instantly.
Lorena
"Research shows visual clutter in bedrooms raises cortisol — your stress hormone. A clean bedroom isn't a luxury. It's affecting how well you sleep every single night."
🛋
Zone 04 · Finish strong
Living Areas & Entryway
Return every homeless item to its designated space
Walk the room with a basket. Collect anything that doesn't belong here.
Dust shelves, TV unit, and decor top to bottom
Remote controls, lamp bases, picture frames — all of it.
Wipe down light switches and door handles
The most touched surfaces in the house. Consistently skipped in most cleans.
Fluff and reset sofa cushions and pillows
Vacuum the sofa surface and under the cushions.
Clean the entryway — floor, hooks, surfaces
This is the first and last thing you see every day. It sets the tone for the whole house.
Vacuum all rugs and carpets
Slow passes cover more than fast ones. Don't rush this step.
Sweep and mop hard floors
Always the last task in the house. You've been walking through every room — finish here.
Lorena
"Living areas collect items from every room in the house. Before you clean a single surface, spend 5 minutes returning things to where they actually live. It changes everything."
The Rule That Changes Everything

Always clean in this order.

Skip the sequence and you'll clean the same surfaces twice. Follow it and you move through the house without backtracking once.

01
Declutter
Return items to their homes before touching a cloth
02
Top to bottom
Dust falls. Always clean high surfaces first
03
Dry to wet
Dust and sweep before any liquid touches a surface
04
Floor last
Every zone. Every time. Without exception.
