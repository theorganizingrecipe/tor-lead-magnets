The process

Here's exactly what
working with us looks like.

Every TOR client goes through the same five steps. We've built it this way because it works — and because you deserve to know what you're committing to before you commit.

1

The Form

Before we meet, you fill out a short form. We ask about your spaces, your goals, and how your days actually run. This way we come to the consultation already prepared — not starting from zero.

You set the agenda
2

The Consultation

We walk through your spaces together. We diagnose the real problem — not just the clutter on the surface, but the routines, the habits, and the moments where things break down. No judgment. Just honest diagnosis.

30 minutes · onsite or virtual
3

The Home Systems Organizing Plan

You receive a PDF proposal with the full scope of work, the investment, and our available dates. Every line item is clear. No hidden fees. No surprises added after the fact.

Written proposal · yours to keep
4

The Session

We show up for a full day. By the end of that day, your primary space is done — not "started," not "mostly there." The system is installed and your family knows how to use it.

50% deposit reserves your dates
5

After: Maintenance, Membership, or Coaching

The session is not the end. We offer ongoing maintenance visits, a membership for clients who want someone to keep the systems running, and coaching for those who want to learn to do it themselves — with guidance.

So the system doesn't fall apart
