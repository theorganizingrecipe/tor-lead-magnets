The TOR Method

5 steps from chaos
to completely organized

Every TOR project follows the same proven framework. Here's what you can expect from the moment you reach out to the final reveal.

1

Free Virtual Consultation (30 min)

We meet on video so Lorena can see your space and understand your lifestyle. No judgment, just an honest conversation about what's working and what isn't. This call is completely free and there's zero pressure to book anything.

⭐ Today's Step
2

Space Assessment & Custom Plan

Lorena puts together a personalized organization plan based on your space, your habits, and your goals. You'll know exactly what we're doing, in what order, and why, before a single item gets moved.

3

Declutter Session

Together, we go through your belongings using The Organizing Recipe method. Lorena guides the decisions, you never have to figure out what to keep alone. It's surprisingly fast, and most clients say it feels like a weight lifting off.

4

Systems & Organization

Once the clutter is gone, we build systems that work for your real life. Labels, containers, zones, and everything is set up so it stays organized without constant maintenance.

5

The Reveal

Walk back into a space that looks and feels completely different. Lorena does a final walkthrough to make sure everything makes sense for you and your family. This is the moment clients always say they wish they'd done sooner.
